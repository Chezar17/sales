Read this first

This guide is general information, not financial, tax, or legal advice. Earnings vary widely by country, skill, time, and luck, and many platforms have waiting periods, minimum payouts, or changing rules. Estimates are typical ranges, not promises. Always read each platform's current terms, and check tax obligations where you live.

  • Never pay anyone to "unlock" work or training. Real platforms do not charge for tasks.
  • Never share your bank PIN, OTP codes, or password with anyone claiming to be a recruiter.
  • Use one account per person, and do not use VPNs on platforms that prohibit them.
  • If a job asks you to deposit money or buy a "starter kit," walk away.
